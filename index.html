Leadership & Executive Profile

Professor Hammad Mohammed Sahil

Chief Executive Officer, Dynamic Features University of London & Upwork Asian Ambassador

Professor Hammad Mohammed Sahil serves as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Dynamic Features University of London and is a Spot Contributor within the Imperial Community, where he holds the distinction of being its youngest senior executive member. In this role, he oversees the institution's collaborations with leading companies and provides strategic guidance on security platforms across the IMP community.

Born on 26 August 2007 in Chittagong, Bangladesh, Professor Sahil began his academic journey at Dynamic Features in 2018. He subsequently attended Government Muslim High School, Chattogram, from 2019 to 2024, followed by Government City College, Chattogram, from 2024 to 2026. Alongside his studies, he began his professional career as a freelancer, achieving recognition as one of the World's Top 100 Freelancers on Upwork in 2020, and currently serves as Upwork's Asian Ambassador, representing the platform's freelance community across Asia.

He joined the IMP community as an Assistant Professor in 2021 and has since advanced to the position of CEO. Under his leadership, more than 127,000 students have gone on to achieve successful career outcomes. He is also the founder of two startup ventures, Excen Co. Macros LTD and XiteX.
